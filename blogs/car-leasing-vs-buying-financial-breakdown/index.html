Leasing vs Buying: The Basics

Leasing a car means paying for its depreciation over a set period, typically 2-3 years. Buying means owning the car outright after paying off the loan.

Each option has advantages depending on your driving habits and financial situation.

Pros and Cons

Leasing Pros

Lower monthly payments, access to newer models, no ownership hassles.

Buying Pros

Build equity, no mileage restrictions, keep the car long-term.
