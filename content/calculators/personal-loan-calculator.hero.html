Personal Loan EMI Calculator — Cost, Tenure, and Consolidation Checks

Personal loans are unsecured, so rates sit above mortgages. Apps often show a tiny EMI by stretching tenure to five or seven years. This tool rebuilds the reducing-balance instalment so you can see interest, not just the monthly bite.

Use it for weddings, medical bills, or credit-card consolidation. A 2% processing fee on a ₹4 lakh loan is ₹8,000 on day one — add that outside the EMI. If you consolidate cards at 16% and then spend on those cards again, you have two debts, not one clever plan.

Example: ₹4,00,000 at 16% for 4 years is about ₹11,300 EMI and well over ₹1.4 lakh of interest. Compare a 24-month vs 48-month tenure here; the shorter one hurts monthly cash and saves a large slice of interest.
