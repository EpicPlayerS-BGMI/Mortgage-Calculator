FD Calculator — Maturity Value with Quarterly Compounding

Fixed deposits (term deposits / CDs) lock a lump sum at a stated rate. CalcBase compounds quarterly: FV = P × (1 + r/4)^(4 × years). Some banks pay monthly; premature withdrawal usually cuts the rate. This estimate assumes you hold to maturity.

Compare the rate with inflation and tax. A 7% FD in a 30% tax slab is 4.9% after tax. If inflation is 6%, real yield is negative. That does not make FDs useless — they are for money you cannot risk, not for 20-year retirement maths.

Example: ₹2,00,000 at 7% for 5 years matures around ₹2.83 lakh. Laddering three FDs of 1, 2, and 3 years keeps some cash coming due without betting the whole corpus on one reset date.
