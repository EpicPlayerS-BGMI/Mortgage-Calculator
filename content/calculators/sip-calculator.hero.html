SIP Calculator — Future Value of a Monthly Investment

A SIP (or dollar-cost average) invests a fixed amount every month. This page projects future value with monthly compounding. The rate you type is an assumption, not a promise — equity funds can finish well below a 12% illustration.

Formula: FV = P × [((1+r)^n − 1) / r] × (1+r), where r is the monthly rate. Expense ratios and taxes are not subtracted. Run 8% and 12% on the same contribution so you see a range, not a single cheerful number.

Example: ₹10,000 a month for 15 years at 12% assumed is ₹18 lakh invested and a much larger projected corpus. At 8% the corpus is smaller and still useful. Money needed inside three years does not belong in an equity SIP — use an FD or liquid fund for that slice.
