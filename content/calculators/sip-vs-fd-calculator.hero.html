SIP vs FD Calculator — Compare the Same Monthly Savings Two Ways

This comparison invests the same monthly amount for the same years: one path as an equity-style SIP with monthly compounding, the other as an FD-style lump of the total invested at an annual rate. It answers “how wide is the gap?”, not “which product is always better?”

SIP can finish below the FD if markets are weak. FD can lose to inflation and tax. Split by date: money needed in 12 months does not belong in equity; money for 15-year retirement rarely belongs only in deposits after tax.

Example: ₹10,000 a month for 10 years is ₹12 lakh invested. At 12% assumed SIP versus 7% FD the SIP line is higher on paper. Drop the SIP assumption to 8% and the gap shrinks. Use both rates before you move an emergency fund into a volatile scheme.
