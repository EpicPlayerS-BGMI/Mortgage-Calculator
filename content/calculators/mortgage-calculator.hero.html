Mortgage Calculator — Payment, Interest, and Tenure Trade-offs

A home loan looks cheap when you only stare at EMI. Stretching tenure from 15 to 30 years can cut the monthly amount while almost doubling lifetime interest. This calculator is for that comparison: down payment versus financed amount, rate, and years.

In India many home loans reset with the repo-linked rate. In the US, tax and insurance often sit on top of principal-and-interest. In the UK, a two-year fix can jump at remortgage. The widget estimates principal and interest only — not stamp duty, PMI, or service charges.

Example: ₹50 lakh at 8.5% for 20 years is about ₹43,400 EMI and roughly ₹54 lakh of interest. The same loan over 15 years costs more each month and far less interest. Run both, then a +1% rate shock, before you treat the lowest EMI as the “best” deal.
