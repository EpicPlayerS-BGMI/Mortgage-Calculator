Car Loan Calculator — Financed Amount, EMI, and Interest

Dealers quote EMI on the on-road price, then quietly add insurance and accessories into the loan. This calculator should be used on the amount you actually finance: on-road price minus down payment, not the sticker on the windshield.

A longer tenure makes the car feel cheaper each month while you pay interest on a depreciating asset. If you might sell in three years, model the remaining principal — that is what you still owe when the car is worth less.

Example: ₹9,00,000 financed at 9.5% for 5 years is about ₹18,900 a month and roughly ₹2.3 lakh of interest. Raising the down payment by ₹1 lakh cuts both EMI and interest. Hypothecation charges and extended warranty sit outside this estimate.
