Investment Calculator — Starting Amount, Monthly Adds, and Compounding

This page models a lump sum that grows monthly plus an optional SIP on the same assumed rate. It is a compounding worksheet, not a stock-market forecast. Haircut the result for taxes and fund expenses before you set a goal.

If the money is for a house in 24 months, do not type 12%. Use a deposit-like rate. If the horizon is 15 years and you can sit through a 30% drawdown, a higher assumed rate is a planning ceiling — then run a lower one so the plan still works in a dull decade.

Example: ₹1,00,000 start plus ₹5,000 a month for 10 years at 10% assumed. You put in ₹7 lakh; the projection is higher. If you cannot leave it untouched, the allocation is too aggressive, not the formula’s fault.
