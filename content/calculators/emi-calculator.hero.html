EMI Calculator — Monthly Instalment, Interest, and Total Cost

Use this page when a lender quotes a monthly figure and you need to know what that EMI is actually made of. CalcBase uses the standard reducing-balance formula — the same family of maths most Indian banks use for home, car, and personal loans — not a “flat rate” advertisement.

Enter principal, annual rate, and tenure. You get monthly EMI, total payment, and total interest. Switch currency in the header to think in rupees, dollars, or pounds. The number is an estimate: it does not include processing fees, GST, insurance, or late-payment charges.

Worked check: ₹3,00,000 at 14% for 36 months is roughly ₹10,250 a month and about ₹69,000 of interest. If a salesperson’s “12% flat” EMI is close to that, they are not using the same method. Compare both in this tool before you sign.
