CalcBase does not require account registration to use its calculators. We do not ask for bank login credentials, government ID numbers, or personal financial account details to generate calculator results.

The values you type into our calculators—such as loan amount, interest rate, tenure, income, or investment assumptions—are processed in your browser to generate results.

We do not require an account to perform these calculations, and we do not intentionally store those financial inputs on our servers as part of normal calculator usage.
