Editorial Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

CalcBase publishes browser-based financial calculators and educational guides for readers in India, the USA, and the UK. This policy explains how we create content, what we will and will not claim, and how readers can request corrections.

Our purpose

We help people understand loan EMIs, mortgages, SIPs, fixed deposits, inflation, and related planning trade-offs before they speak with a bank, broker, or adviser. Articles should teach formulas, assumptions, and practical decision steps—not push products or create fear.

Who writes and reviews content

CalcBase is independently operated by founder Shivam, with support from a small editorial and development team. Pages may be bylined as “By Shivam, CalcBase” or “By CalcBase Editorial” when the team collaborates on research and drafting.

Calculator logic is checked against standard amortisation, EMI, and compounding formulas documented on our Methodology page. Educational articles are reviewed for clarity, internal consistency, and regional caveats before publication.

Research and originality standards

  • Each guide must add original examples, tables, or decision frameworks—not only rewritten definitions.
  • We avoid identical section templates across articles so every page stands alone as useful reading.
  • Country notes for India, USA, and UK are included when rules, products, or naming conventions differ.
  • We do not invent bank rates, government thresholds, or personalised offers as if they were current official quotes.
  • Worked numbers are labelled as educational illustrations and may use rounded figures for teaching.

What CalcBase is not

CalcBase is not a bank, NBFC, mortgage broker, registered investment adviser, tax firm, or law practice. Nothing on this site is personalised financial, tax, legal, or investment advice. Always confirm final figures with a qualified professional and the institution offering the product.

Full legal wording lives on our Disclaimer and Terms pages.

Updates and corrections

Finance rules, tax treatments, and product structures change. We update pages when formulas, explanations, or regional notes need improvement, and we date major policy pages. If you find an error in a calculator result or article, email support@calcbase.tech with the page URL, the expected result, and your inputs. Material corrections are prioritised.

Advertising and reader experience

CalcBase may display ads through Google AdSense and similar partners after approval. Advertising must not disguise itself as editorial advice. Calculator inputs stay in your browser for estimates; ad and analytics partners may use cookies as described in our Privacy Policy.

We aim for a readable layout: clear navigation, working calculators, accessible contact details, and content that remains useful even if ads are blocked.
